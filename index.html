Questions

Before you ask.

How long does a website take?

A simple business site is usually live in 7 to 10 days once we have your content. Bigger sites and stores take longer. E-commerce and custom apps are scoped per project. We give you a date in writing before we start.

What does a website cost?

It depends entirely on scope. A five-page business site and a booking engine are different pieces of work. We quote after the discovery call, in writing, as one fixed number with nothing hidden underneath it. Domain and hosting are billed separately at actual cost.

What do you need from me?

Your logo if you have one, photos of your work or products, and a short brief on what you do. If you don't have content ready, we can write it with you.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. We hand over an admin panel and a short walkthrough video for the parts you'll want to change: prices, packages, photos, blog posts. Anything structural, we handle on a care plan.

Do you handle domain and hosting?

We set both up and manage them for you. They're billed at actual cost with no markup, so you always own your domain and can move it any time.

Will it show up on Google?

Every site is built with clean structure, fast loading, sitemaps and Google Business Profile linkage. That gets you indexed and competitive locally. Aggressive ranking in a crowded market is ongoing SEO work, which we quote separately.

What happens after launch?

You get 30 days of free fixes for anything that isn't working as agreed. After that, most clients stay on a monthly care plan for updates, backups and small edits.
